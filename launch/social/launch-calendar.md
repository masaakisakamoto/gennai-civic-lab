# v0.7 Launch Calendar

This calendar is designed for Japan-based posting in JST.

## Recommended window

- X: Tuesday to Thursday morning or early afternoon
- Instagram: Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon to evening
- Soft launch: Sunday evening or Monday afternoon

## Suggested sequence

### T-1 day

- Confirm GitHub Actions are green.
- Create tag `v0.7.0`.
- Prepare screenshots from Local Runner.
- Replace `<YOUR_GITHUB_URL>` in social drafts.

### Launch day morning

- Post short X announcement.
- Reply to your own post with a technical thread.

### Launch day afternoon/evening

- Post Instagram carousel.
- Pin or highlight the GitHub URL.

### T+1 day

- Post a follow-up with concrete learnings:
  - what worked
  - what failed
  - next roadmap

## Draft metrics to track

- GitHub stars
- profile visits
- reposts / bookmarks
- comments from civic-tech / engineering audiences
- inbound collaboration messages
