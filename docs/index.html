Open-source civic AI lab

Safe, auditable AI for civic and public use.

gennai-civic-lab is an open-source project for building small, explainable, and human-centered AI applications for civic, educational, and public-sector contexts.

View on GitHub Read in Japanese

What it includes

Building blocks for experimenting with public-facing AI systems while keeping safety, auditability, evaluation, and human review in the loop.

Easy Japanese rewriting

Rewrite civic and administrative text into clearer, more accessible language.

Citizen FAQ RAG

Answer citizen questions using local FAQ data and retrieval-augmented generation.

Local Runner

Run and inspect AI app outputs locally with previews, payloads, and curl export.

Audit primitives

Record privacy-aware audit events without storing raw personal input.

Evaluation reports

Generate reports to review quality, safety, and expected behavior.

Red-team smoke tests

Check basic safety boundaries before exposing AI flows to real users.

Start small. Make it explainable. Keep it auditable.

Public AI systems should not be black boxes. They should be tested carefully, reviewed by humans, and improved through transparent, reproducible workflows.

Who it is for

Designed as a bridge between engineering, civic practice, education, and responsible AI experimentation.

Civic and public-sector teams

Explore how AI can support public communication, citizen FAQs, local services, and internal learning without jumping too quickly into production.

Educators

Use it as a practical learning environment for AI literacy, RAG, safety, and evaluation.

Developers

Study small, auditable patterns for building public-facing AI applications.

Researchers and builders

Extend the lab with new civic AI apps, safer workflows, and evaluation-first design patterns.
